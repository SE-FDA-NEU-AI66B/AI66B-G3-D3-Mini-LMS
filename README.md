## P0 stories
### [[#9]](https://github.com/SE-FDA-NEU-AI66B/AI66B-G3-D3-Mini-LMS/issues/9) US1 - Lecturer creates a quiz
Login as **instructor** → In **My quizzes**, click `New quiz` in the top right → Fill the form → Click `Create quiz` → `Add question` at least one → Click `Save changes`/`Publish`.
### [[#10]](https://github.com/SE-FDA-NEU-AI66B/AI66B-G3-D3-Mini-LMS/issues/10) US2 - Student sees available quizzes
Login as **student** → Available **Quizzes** are displayed in the **Dashboard**.
### [[#11]](https://github.com/SE-FDA-NEU-AI66B/AI66B-G3-D3-Mini-LMS/issues/11) US3 - Student takes a quiz with countdown timer
Login as **student** → Click `Take` an available quiz.
### [[#12]](https://github.com/SE-FDA-NEU-AI66B/AI66B-G3-D3-Mini-LMS/issues/12) US4 - Automatic grading and immediate score
Login as **student** → Click `Take` an available quiz → Do the quiz → Click `Submit quiz` or wait until the **timer countdowns**.
### [[#14]](https://github.com/SE-FDA-NEU-AI66B/AI66B-G3-D3-Mini-LMS/issues/14) US5 - Lecturer publishes / unpublishes a quiz
Login as **instructor** → In **My quizzes**, toggle `Publish`/`Unpublish` buttons of the chosen quizzes.
### [[#17]](https://github.com/SE-FDA-NEU-AI66B/AI66B-G3-D3-Mini-LMS/issues/17) US6 - Admin manages user accounts
Login as **admin** → In **Accounts**:  
*Create an account*: click `New account` in the top right.  
*Update an account*: click `Edit` button of the chosen account.  
*Delete an account*: click `Delete` button of the chosen account.  
*Filter accounts*: The **filter panel** on top.
### [[#54]](https://github.com/SE-FDA-NEU-AI66B/AI66B-G3-D3-Mini-LMS/issues/54) US11 - Admin creates courses and assigns lecturers
Login as **admin** → In **Courses**, click `New course` in the top right → Fill the form and **assign an instructor** → click `Save course`.
### [[#55]](https://github.com/SE-FDA-NEU-AI66B/AI66B-G3-D3-Mini-LMS/issues/55) US12 - Admin enrolls students into a course
Login as **admin** → In **Courses**, click `Rosters` of the chosen course → Click `Add student` on top of the dialog → Enter **student email** → Click `Add to course`.
